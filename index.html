How to choose a game

Think of three things: where the players are, how old they are and how much time you have. For members in different cities, choose a WhatsApp group game. For a living room full of relatives, a party game such as dumb charades or passing the parcel works for all ages. For children with energy to burn, go outside with a traditional game. Keep one quiet game ready for the time when everyone is tired.

Questions people ask

Which game is best for a quiet WhatsApp group?

Start with a game that needs one tap or one word: This or That, Would You Rather or Emoji Guess. Once people start replying, move to a longer game such as Name, Place, Animal, Thing.

How do I keep a WhatsApp game fair?

Post clear rules first, fix a time limit, and for quiz-type games count only the first correct reply or ask members to answer in one message. One host should be the judge, and the host's decision is final.

Are these games safe for family groups?

Yes. Every list of questions, tasks and words on this site is written to be clean and kind, suitable for a group that has children and elders in it.

Do I need to install anything?

No. The games are explained on web pages, and the Tambola caller and team maker work in your browser. Nothing is installed and there is no login.
