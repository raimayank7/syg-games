Easy riddles for kids with answers

Twelve very easy riddles for young children. The answers are things they see every day, so they can shout them out with confidence.

  1. I have four legs but I cannot walk.
    You put your plate and your homework on me.

    Show answer

    A table

  2. I rise in the east and wake up the world.
    I am too bright to look at.

    Show answer

    The Sun

  3. I have a face and two hands,
    but no arms and no legs.
    I tell you when it is time for school.

    Show answer

    A clock

  4. I fall from the sky but I never get hurt.
    I fill the ponds and make the frogs sing.

    Show answer

    Rain

  5. I have a tail of paper and I dance on a string.
    The wind is my best friend.

    Show answer

    A kite

  6. We are two brothers who always go out together.
    You tie us to your feet.

    Show answer

    Shoes

  7. I have many pages and a hard or soft cover.
    Open me and I tell you a story.

    Show answer

    A book

  8. I say "moo" and I eat grass.
    The milk in your glass comes from me.

    Show answer

    A cow

  9. I am round and full of air.
    Kick me and I fly towards the goal.

    Show answer

    A football

  10. I have two wheels, a seat and a bell.
    Push my pedals and off we go.

    Show answer

    A bicycle

  11. I am cold and sweet.
    Lick me fast, or I will melt down your hand.

    Show answer

    An ice cream

  12. I light up the night but I am not a lamp.
    Some nights I am round and some nights I am thin.

    Show answer

    The Moon
