Atlas

Atlas is the place-name version of antakshari. Each player names a place that begins with the last letter of the place named before.

Players
2 or more
Time
10 to 20 minutes
You need
Nothing
Ages
9 and above
Type
Travel and family games

Who it suits and when to play

Atlas suits players of nine and above who know a fair number of place names, and it is a classic of train journeys and school buses. Two can play, or a whole compartment. Each place must begin with the last letter of the one before. It quietly teaches geography, and families often keep a map or atlas at hand to settle doubts.

How to play

  1. The first player names any country, state, city or river, for example "India".
  2. The next player names a place beginning with its last letter: India ends in A, so "Agra".
  3. No place may be repeated in the same game.
  4. A player who cannot answer in ten seconds is out. The last player left wins.

Tips and variations

  • Too many places end in "A". Keep a few "A" places in mind: Amritsar, Assam, Agartala, Alwar, Ajmer, Aizawl, Ambala.
  • Stricter version: only Indian places, or only cities.

Common problems and how to fix them

  • Everything ends in A. Allow names of rivers, mountains and lakes too, or play by the last sound instead of the last letter.
  • A player invents a place. Anyone may challenge. If the place cannot be found on a map later, the player loses a life.
  • The game never ends. Give each player three lives, and ten seconds to answer.
