Guess the Member

Members send the host one little-known, harmless fact about themselves. The host posts the facts one by one and the group guesses who it belongs to. A lovely game for family and old-friends groups.

Players
5 or more
Time
15 minutes
You need
Nothing
Ages
10 and above
Type
WhatsApp group games

How to play

  1. A day before, ask every member to send you privately one fun fact about themselves that few people know.
  2. At game time, post the facts one at a time, numbered.
  3. Members reply with the name they guess. One guess per person.
  4. Reveal the owner. Everyone who guessed right gets a point.
  5. The owner may add the short story behind the fact.

Ready message to start the game

Guess the member! One of us sent me this fact: "I once won a prize for the best handwriting in school." Who is it? One guess each!

Tips and variations

  • Childhood photos work the same way: collect them privately and post them one by one.
  • Post only facts and photos that the owner has agreed to share with the whole group.
