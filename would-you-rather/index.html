Would You Rather

Post a funny choice between two things and ask everyone to pick one and say why. There are no right answers, which is why even shy members join in.

Players
2 or more
Time
10 to 20 minutes
You need
Nothing
Ages
6 and above
Type
WhatsApp group games

How to play

  1. Post one question. WhatsApp polls work well: put the two choices as poll options.
  2. Everyone votes and gives a one-line reason.
  3. The host reads out the funniest reason, or the group reacts to it with an emoji.
  4. Go on to the next question. Ten questions make a good round.

Ready message to start the game

Would you rather... A) have summer holidays twice a year, or B) have no homework ever? Reply A or B and tell us why!

Would you rather questions

  1. Would you rather be able to fly, or be able to become invisible?
  2. Would you rather eat only sweets for a week, or only spicy food for a week?
  3. Would you rather live in the mountains, or by the sea?
  4. Would you rather have a pet elephant, or a pet dolphin?
  5. Would you rather never use a phone for a month, or never watch TV for a year?
  6. Would you rather always be ten minutes late, or always be one hour early?
  7. Would you rather talk to animals, or speak every language in the world?
  8. Would you rather have summer all year, or winter all year?
  9. Would you rather cook dinner every day, or wash the dishes every day?
  10. Would you rather travel 100 years into the past, or 100 years into the future?
  11. Would you rather have a rewind button for your life, or a pause button?
  12. Would you rather eat chai without sugar forever, or samosas without filling forever?
  13. Would you rather be the funniest person in the room, or the smartest?
  14. Would you rather wake up at 5 am every day, or stay up till 2 am every day?
  15. Would you rather go to school in pyjamas, or in a wedding dress?
  16. Would you rather have a personal cook, or a personal driver?
  17. Would you rather lose your keys every week, or your charger every week?
  18. Would you rather only whisper, or only shout?
  19. Would you rather give a speech to 1,000 people, or sing in front of 100?
  20. Would you rather have rain every evening, or never see rain again?
  21. Would you rather read minds, or see one day into the future?
  22. Would you rather live without mangoes, or without ice cream?
  23. Would you rather walk everywhere, or cycle everywhere?
  24. Would you rather be a famous cricketer, or a famous scientist?
  25. Would you rather always tell the truth, or never speak again?
  26. Would you rather have a house with a secret room, or a garden with a treehouse?
  27. Would you rather win a small prize every day, or one big prize once?
  28. Would you rather visit the Moon, or the bottom of the ocean?
  29. Would you rather have hiccups for a day, or a sneeze that never comes for a day?
  30. Would you rather meet your great-grandparents, or your great-grandchildren?

Tips and variations

  • Let the member with the funniest reason ask the next question.
  • Keep the choices light. Skip anything about looks, money or family matters.
