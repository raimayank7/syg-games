Blindfold Makeover

One partner is blindfolded and must put a bindi, lipstick, kajal-free "eyeshadow" dots and bangles on the other, guided only by touch and by the giggled directions of the audience. The results are gloriously wrong. A favourite at kitty parties, bridal showers and mehndi evenings, and very good when husbands are made to do it.

Ages
12 and up
Players
4 to 20, in pairs
Time
10 to 15 minutes
Place
seated
Energy
quiet
Group size
Small group (4 to 8), Medium group (9 to 20)
Good for
Teenagers, Adults, Ladies' parties, Couples

What you need

For each pair: a scarf for the blindfold, a small kit of sticker bindis, a lipstick or lip crayon, hair clips, a few bangles, a mirror for afterwards, wipes.

How to play

  1. Form pairs. One sits in a chair as the model; the other stands in front, blindfolded.
  2. Lay each kit on a tray within the blindfolded player's reach.
  3. On "Go", the blindfolded players have three minutes to make their partners "party ready": bindi in the middle of the forehead, lipstick on the lips, clips in the hair, bangles on the wrists.
  4. The model may not speak or help. The audience may shout whatever it likes.
  5. At "Stop", blindfolds come off and the mirror goes round.

Who wins

The audience votes by clapping for the best makeover, and separately for the funniest.

Variations and tips

  • Reverse the roles for a second round.
  • Children's version: stickers and hair clips only.
  • Saree or dupatta draping blindfolded, over the model's clothes, is a variation for ladies' parties.

Safety and kindness

Nothing goes near the eyes: no kajal, no mascara, no eyeliner. Use each model's own lipstick, or new sealed lip crayons and stickers, never shared ones. Ask models about skin allergies. Models must agree to photographs before any are taken or posted.

Common problems and how to fix them

  • Models keep guiding their partners. A model who speaks or moves a hand loses a point.
  • Someone does not want make-up on their face. Offer the role of judge or timekeeper.
