Cup Pyramid

Players race to build a pyramid of paper cups and then collapse it back into a single stack. Hands move in a blur, towers fall at the last moment, and the crowd groans. It is a one-minute game that people ask to play again at once, and it makes a good head-to-head final.

Ages
6 and up
Players
2 to 20
Time
5 to 10 minutes
Place
a table or the floor
Energy
active
Group size
Small group (4 to 8), Medium group (9 to 20)
Good for
Children, Teenagers, Adults, All ages together, Office

What you need

Ten paper or plastic cups of the same size for each player. A flat surface. A timer.

How to play

  1. Give each player a stack of ten cups, mouth down.
  2. On "Go", each player builds a pyramid with four cups in the bottom row, then three, two and one.
  3. As soon as the pyramid stands complete, the player takes it down again, sliding the cups back into one stack.
  4. Cups that fall may be picked up and replaced, but the clock keeps running.
  5. A player's time stops when the single stack is back on the table with both hands off it.

Who wins

The fastest time wins. In a race between two players, the first to finish wins.

Variations and tips

  • Bigger: fifteen cups in rows of five, four, three, two and one.
  • Team relay: one member builds, the next takes down, the third builds again.
  • One-handed, or with a balloon held between the knees, for people who have found it too easy.
  • Tower: who can build the tallest free-standing tower of cups in one minute?

Safety and kindness

Use paper cups you can reuse for serving water later in the party, or recycle afterwards.

Common problems and how to fix them

  • Flimsy cups stick together. Separate and loosen every stack before the game.
  • Disputes about who finished first. Race two at a time, each with a judge.

Party plans that use this game

  • Birthday party, ages 7 to 10
  • Office party or team outing
  • Small flat or rainy day

Host tools

Time the round with the party timer, make chits or random teams with the chit maker, and keep the points on the scoreboard.
