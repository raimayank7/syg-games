Birthday party plan for ages 7 to 10

For
10 to 20 children
Length
About two hours including food
Games
8

This is the golden age for party games. Children of seven to ten love rules, love teams and love to win, and they can wait for a turn. They also have endless energy, so the plan alternates running games with table challenges, and puts the messiest and loudest game just before the food, when a wash is due anyway.

The running order

TimeGameNotes for the host
0 to 10 minFire in the MountainA fast opener that mixes the children who do not know each other.
10 to 25 minDog and the BoneMake two teams here and keep them for the rest of the party.
25 to 35 minCup PyramidTeam relay at two tables. Count it for team points.
35 to 45 minStraw and RajmaOne minute, four players at a time, others cheer.
45 to 55 minWrap the MummyThree wrappers and one mummy per team. Take the photographs now.
55 to 65 minHanging Jalebi RaceUse ring biscuits if you prefer. Then hands washed.
65 to 90 minBreak or activityCake and food.
90 to 105 minRaja Mantri Chor SipahiA calm seated game while the food settles; play in groups of four or five.
105 to 115 minBalloon StompThe grand finale, outdoors if possible. Then return gifts.

Timings are a guide. Stop any game while people still want more, and drop one if you are running late.

What to get ready

  • Chalk or rope for two lines, and a soft "bone"
  • Paper cups: ten per team
  • Straws, dry rajma, plates and bowls
  • Toilet rolls: two per team
  • A rope, thread and jalebis or ring biscuits
  • Chits for Raja Mantri
  • Balloons and string, with spares
  • A score chart on the wall

Running it smoothly

  • Keep teams all party long and put a score chart where they can see it. Add points for cheering and for tidying up.
  • Explain by showing. Play one slow demonstration round instead of listing rules.
  • Appoint helpers. A child who dislikes rough games makes an excellent scorer or timekeeper.
  • Prizes: small and the same for the whole winning team; a consolation for the others.

Safety notes

Check food allergies with parents before the jalebi race. Flat closed shoes for Balloon Stomp. Do the running games on grass or a non-slip floor.

The games in this plan

  • Fire in the Mountain 10 min
  • Dog and the Bone 15 min
  • Cup Pyramid 5 to 10 min
  • Straw and Rajma 5 to 10 min
  • Wrap the Mummy 10 min
  • Hanging Jalebi Race 10 min
  • Raja Mantri Chor Sipahi 15 to 20 min
  • Balloon Stomp 5 to 10 min

On the day
