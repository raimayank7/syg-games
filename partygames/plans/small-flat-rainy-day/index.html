Small flat and rainy-day plan

For
4 to 10 people in one room
Length
About 75 minutes
Games
6

A party in a small flat needs games that happen on a table or in a circle of chairs, that do not send anyone running into the furniture, and that the neighbours downstairs will not hear. The same list rescues a birthday when the rain has cancelled the park. Everything here uses things from the kitchen and a few sheets of paper.

The running order

TimeGameNotes for the host
0 to 15 minRaja Mantri Chor SipahiAt the dining table.
15 to 25 minStraw and RajmaTwo or three at a time.
25 to 35 minCup PyramidHead to head on the table, with a stopwatch.
35 to 45 minBiscuit on the ForeheadEveryone at once, seated on the sofa.
45 to 60 minCut the Flour CakeOn a tray. Have the towel ready.
60 to 75 minWink DetectiveWith eight or more; with fewer, play Don't Say It.

Timings are a guide. Stop any game while people still want more, and drop one if you are running late.

What to get ready

  • Chits and a pen
  • Straws, rajma, plates and bowls
  • Twenty paper cups
  • Round biscuits
  • Flour, a bowl, a tray, a blunt knife, a wrapped toffee
  • A timer on a phone

Small-space rules

  • Clear one table and one patch of floor before guests come; put breakables in another room.
  • Sit-down games only, with one stand-up stretch in the middle.
  • Messy games on a tray or over a spread newspaper.
  • Mind the hour. Quiet games after nine at night; your neighbours will thank you.
  • Fewer people, more turns. With six guests everyone plays every round, so shorter games feel right.

More games that need only paper and a pen are on our neighbour site Board Games: dots and boxes, battleships and five in a row.

The games in this plan

  • Raja Mantri Chor Sipahi 15 to 20 min
  • Straw and Rajma 5 to 10 min
  • Cup Pyramid 5 to 10 min
  • Biscuit on the Forehead 5 min
  • Cut the Flour Cake 10 min
  • Wink Detective 15 min
