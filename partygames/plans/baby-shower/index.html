Baby shower (godh bharai) games plan

For
10 to 30 guests, mostly seated
Length
About one hour
Games
4

At a baby shower the guest of honour should be seated, comfortable and at the centre of every game without having to do anything tiring. Choose games played from chairs, keep them short, and avoid anything with strong smells, loud bangs or teasing about her body. The games below let friends and both families share stories and good wishes.

The running order

TimeGameNotes for the host
0 to 15 minHow Well Do You Know the Bride?Played as "How well do you know the mum-to-be?" Ask about cravings, the nursery colour, lullabies she knows.
15 to 25 minStick the BindiAs "stick the dummy on the baby" or "the bindi on the teddy", for the children and aunts.
25 to 40 minHum That TuneLullabies and children's songs, old and new.
40 to 50 minDon't Say ItBaby words: rattle, nappy, lori, cradle.
50 to 60 minBreak or activityWishes for the baby: every guest writes one line of advice or blessing on a card. The mother reads a few aloud and keeps them all.

Timings are a guide. Stop any game while people still want more, and drop one if you are running late.

What to get ready

  • Her answers, collected beforehand
  • A picture and stickers
  • Song slips
  • Word cards
  • Cards and pens for wishes, and a box to keep them in
  • A comfortable chair, water and a footstool for the mother-to-be

Kind hosting

  • Ask her first which games she would enjoy, and whether she wants photographs shared.
  • No guessing her size or weight, no tasting of baby food, no strong smells.
  • No balloons bursting.
  • Keep it short. An hour of games is plenty; she will tire before the guests do.
  • Include both grandmothers-to-be as judges or storytellers: "what was the father like as a baby?" is a game in itself.

The games in this plan

  • How Well Do You Know the Bride? 15 min
  • Stick the Bindi 10 to 15 min
  • Hum That Tune 20 min
  • Don't Say It 20 min
