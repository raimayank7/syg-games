Kitty party plan

For
8 to 20 members
Length
About two hours with lunch or tea
Games
5

A kitty party runs on three things: a punctuality prize, one or two "written" or table games, and one game that gets everyone out of their chairs. The hostess usually wants games that are new to the group, quick to explain, and playable in sarees and good clothes. These fit that brief, and all use things already in the house.

The running order

TimeGameNotes for the host
0 to 10 minBreak or activityPunctuality game: every member who arrives on time draws a chit; one chit carries the prize. Or give a ticket for each five minutes early.
10 to 25 minStraw and RajmaFour at a time, one minute each. Fast and funny.
25 to 40 minCoin in the TumblerEveryone gets five coins. Good while tea is served.
40 to 60 minBlindfold MakeoverPairs. The game people will photograph and talk about.
60 to 75 minBiscuit on the ForeheadAll together, standing.
75 to 105 minBreak or activityLunch or high tea.
105 to 125 minDon't Say ItTwo teams, cards written by the hostess beforehand on the theme of the month.

Timings are a guide. Stop any game while people still want more, and drop one if you are running late.

What to get ready

  • Chits for the punctuality draw
  • Straws, rajma, plates and bowls
  • A bucket, steel glass and coins
  • Kits of sticker bindis, clips and bangles; scarves
  • A packet of light round biscuits
  • Cards for Don't Say It
  • Small wrapped prizes

Hosting notes

  • Theme it. A colour, a festival or a decade of film songs gives the card games their content and the photographs their look.
  • Prizes need not be costly: something useful for the kitchen or the handbag, wrapped well.
  • Keep scores light. One prize per game and a lucky-draw prize for someone who won nothing.
  • No game with food on good clothes unless napkins are ready.
  • Housie is the traditional closer; our neighbour site has a free Tambola caller and ticket maker.

We suggest playing for small gifts and fun, not for money.

The games in this plan

  • Straw and Rajma 5 to 10 min
  • Coin in the Tumbler 10 min
  • Blindfold Makeover 10 to 15 min
  • Biscuit on the Forehead 5 min
  • Don't Say It 20 min

On the day
