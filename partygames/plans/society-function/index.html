Society and colony function plan

For
50 to 200 residents of all ages
Length
Two to three hours, on a lawn or in a hall
Games
6

A large function cannot be run as one game for everybody. The method that works is to split by age group and run several things at once: a stage for games with an audience, a races area, and a few "stall" games that people can try whenever they like. With a volunteer in charge of each, the anchor only has to keep the timetable.

The running order

TimeGameNotes for the host
0 to 20 minBreak or activityArrival: stall games open. Coin in the Tumbler, Stick the Bindi and Cup Pyramid each run by one volunteer, with a small prize for the best score of the day.
20 to 40 minKokla ChhapakiUnder-eights on the lawn, with Poshampa to follow. Two volunteers.
40 to 60 minDog and the BoneAges 8 to 14, in house or block teams.
60 to 75 minHanging Jalebi RaceHeats by age group, including a senior citizens' heat. The crowd gathers for this.
75 to 95 minNewspaper DanceOn stage: couples and parent-child pairs.
95 to 115 minHum That TuneOn stage: block against block, with the audience allowed to shout a bonus answer.
115 to 130 minSeated Balloon VolleyballSenior citizens against the committee.
130 to 145 minBreak or activityPrize distribution and a group photograph.

Timings are a guide. Stop any game while people still want more, and drop one if you are running late.

What to get ready

  • A volunteer for every game and one for first aid
  • A microphone and speaker
  • A printed timetable at the gate
  • Chalk lines, rope and soft cloths
  • A rope and jalebis for forty
  • Newspapers, balloons, song slips
  • Prizes sorted and labelled by game
  • Drinking water, shade and chairs for elders

Organising a crowd

  • Register on arrival. A sticker of a different colour for each age group makes heats easy to call.
  • Run two things at once, never five. People cannot watch everything.
  • Block or wing teams give neighbours a side to cheer for.
  • Senior citizens first. Seat them near the stage and give them their own events early.
  • Safety: a first-aid box, water, shade, and a marked boundary for running games away from the stalls.
  • Prizes: many small ones are better than a few large ones.

The games in this plan

  • Kokla Chhapaki 10 to 15 min
  • Dog and the Bone 15 min
  • Hanging Jalebi Race 10 min
  • Newspaper Dance 10 to 15 min
  • Hum That Tune 20 min
  • Seated Balloon Volleyball 15 min
