Emoji Guess

The quizmaster posts a few emojis and the group guesses the dish, place or festival they stand for. It is the easiest game to start in a quiet group, because everyone can try and wrong guesses are funny too.

Players
3 or more
Time
10 to 20 minutes
You need
Only your phones
Ages
8 and above
Type
WhatsApp group games

How to play

  1. Tell the group the topic, for example "Guess the Indian dish".
  2. Post one emoji puzzle at a time, with its number.
  3. The first member to type the right answer gets one point. Spelling mistakes are fine.
  4. If nobody gets it in two minutes, give a hint, such as the first letter.
  5. After ten puzzles, announce the winner and let them set the next round.

Tips and variations

  • Make your own puzzles from the names of your group members, your town or your school subjects.
  • For a fair game in a big group, ask everyone to send answers to you privately and post the scores later.
