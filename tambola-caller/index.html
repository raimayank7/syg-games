Tambola number caller and ticket maker

Call numbers from 1 to 90 at random, with no repeats, and print fresh Tambola (Housie) tickets. Free, with no app and no login.

–

Each ticket has 3 rows with 5 numbers in each row, in the usual columns (1 to 9, 10 to 19 ... 80 to 90). To play on WhatsApp, take a screenshot of each ticket and send one to each player.

How to use the caller

  1. Give every player a ticket. Make and print them above, or send screenshots.
  2. Tap "Call next number". The big number is the one to announce. The board keeps every number called so far.
  3. When someone claims a prize, check their ticket against the green numbers on the board.
  4. The game is saved in this browser, so the board is still there if you close the page by mistake. Tap "New game" to clear it.

Winning patterns

PatternWhat the player needs
Early fiveAny five numbers on the ticket
Four cornersThe first and last numbers of the top and bottom rows
Top lineAll five numbers of the first row
Middle lineAll five numbers of the second row
Bottom lineAll five numbers of the third row
Full houseAll fifteen numbers on the ticket
