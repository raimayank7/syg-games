How to stop fake forwards in your group

Most false messages are shared by good people who wanted to help. That is why scolding does not work. What works is a simple habit of checking, and an admin who corrects calmly and quickly.

Five checks before you forward

  1. Does it tell you to "forward to everyone"? Real warnings from the police, banks or hospitals do not ask for that.
  2. Does it name a source you can open? "A doctor said" or "received from a senior officer" is not a source.
  3. Is it marked "Forwarded many times"? WhatsApp adds that label to messages that have travelled through many chats. It means the sender did not write it.
  4. Does it make you angry or afraid at once? Messages built to do that deserve a second look.
  5. Can you find it on a trusted news site or the organisation's own site? If you cannot find it in two minutes, do not send it.

Never forward these

  • Medicines, doses and "cures". Ask a doctor.
  • Photos or names of a "kidnapper", "thief" or "suspect". Such rumours have led to innocent people being attacked.
  • Offers of free recharge, gifts or jobs with a link.
  • Any message asking for an OTP, a PIN or a "small payment to receive money".
  • Anything that blames a whole community for something.

What an admin can do

  1. Put "No unverified forwards" in the rules and the description.
  2. When a false message appears, reply to it once, politely, with the correction, then delete it for everyone.
  3. Message the sender privately. Thank them for meaning well and show them the checks above.
  4. For repeat cases, restrict sending to admins for a day, or remove the member for a short time.
