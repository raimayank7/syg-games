Which kind of poll, and when

The polls on this page are of three kinds. Decision polls settle something real: a date, a place, a budget, a rule. Use them instead of a long discussion, state clearly that the majority decides, and then follow the result. Fun polls have no consequence (the best snack, morning person or night owl) and are the gentlest way to wake a silent group, because even the shyest member will tap an option. Check-in polls ask how people are, or what they want more of in the group; they tell an admin things nobody would say in words.

Keeping polls fair

For a decision poll, include every reasonable option, including "any is fine" and, where it applies, "cannot come". Do not add your own opinion in the question. Do not run a poll on a matter that is not really open, such as a rule the committee has already decided; people can tell, and they stop voting. And never use a poll to judge a person. "Who talks the most here?" is funny for everyone except the winner.

How to make a poll in WhatsApp

  1. Open the group and tap the attach button (the paperclip or the plus sign).
  2. Choose "Poll".
  3. Type the question and the options. You can allow one answer or several.
  4. Send it. Everyone votes with one tap, and the result updates live.

This site is independent and is not connected with WhatsApp. Menu names in the app change from time to time; the settings described here were checked in November 2026. For the exact steps on your phone, see the WhatsApp Help Centre at faq.whatsapp.com.

Tips for polls that get votes

  • Keep to four options or fewer.
  • Give a closing time: "Voting closes at 8 pm."
  • Announce the result and act on it. A poll that is ignored teaches people not to vote.
