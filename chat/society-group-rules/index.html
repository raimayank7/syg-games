Housing society group rules

Rules for an apartment or colony group. Societies fight more on WhatsApp than at meetings; these rules keep the group for information.

*Group rules* 1. This group is for society matters only: water, power, security, maintenance and events. 2. Report a problem with the flat number, the place and a photo. One message, not ten. 3. Complaints go to the committee or the office. The group is for information, not for hearings. 4. No personal remarks about any resident, staff member or committee member. 5. No politics, religion, forwards, jokes or good morning messages. Use the social group for those. 6. Advertisements only on the day fixed by the admin, one message per business. 7. Do not post any person's photo or video without consent, including staff and delivery workers. 8. Lost and found, blood needed and safety alerts are always welcome. 9. Committee decisions are announced here. Discuss them at the meeting. 10. Two warnings, then removal for a month. The rule is the same for everybody.

Why societies fight more on the phone than in meetings

In a meeting, people see each other's faces, speak in turn and go home. In a group chat, a complaint about parking is read by two hundred neighbours at once, at any hour, without tone of voice, and five people reply before the first one has finished typing. Small matters become public trials. The rules on this page are built to turn the group back into a notice board and to send disputes to the place where they can actually be solved: the committee.

Getting the rules accepted

Rules posted by one admin look like one neighbour bossing the others. Have the managing committee agree to them, mention that in the first line, and post them from the committee's side. Repeat them when new residents join, and keep a short version in the group description. Apply them first to committee members themselves; nothing builds acceptance faster.

Two groups work better than one

Many societies find peace by splitting: one "Notices" group where only admins post (water, lift, maintenance dates, emergencies), and one open "Residents chat" for everything else. People who want only information mute the second. Buying and selling can move to a third group with its own buy and sell rules.

These rules are also available in हिंदी. For the day an argument breaks out anyway, keep this guide ready.

How to use these rules

  1. Copy the rules and change any line that does not fit your group.
  2. Post them in the group and pin the message, so that it stays at the top.
  3. Put the one-line version in the group description. Ready lines are on the descriptions page.
  4. When a rule is broken, remind the person privately first. See how to be a good admin.
