Travel group names

Names for the trip planning group, whether the trip has happened, is happening or has been "almost final" for three years.

Tap a name to copy it. Then open your group, tap its name at the top and paste.

Make a name with your own word

One trip, or a lifetime of trips?

Decide first what kind of group this is. A group for a single trip should say where and when: "Manali, June: Bags Packed". Everybody will know at a glance which trip a message belongs to, and the group can be left politely when the photos have been shared. A group of people who travel together again and again needs a name that will still fit after five trips, so name the habit, not the place: "Trip Kab Hai?", "Window Seat Club".

Family trips and friends' trips call for different tones. With parents and children along, choose something everyone can say aloud at a hotel reception. With college friends, the long-delayed plan is itself the joke.

Practical points

If a travel agent, driver or homestay owner is added to the group for coordination, keep the name plain and do not put dates of absence and home addresses together in the group's name or description; that is information about an empty house. Avoid names that leave someone out ("Goa Boys") if the group may widen later. And do not name a group after a place before the tickets are booked, unless you enjoy being reminded.

Before the trip, a poll settles dates and budgets faster than fifty messages. After it, the conversation starters keep the group alive until the next plan.

How to choose a good group name

  • Say what the group is. A new member should understand it from the name.
  • Keep it short. Long names are cut off in the chat list; about 25 characters show fully.
  • Make it yours. Add a surname, a city, a year or a flat block: "Sharma Parivaar Express", "Block B Buzz".
  • Keep it kind. A name that makes fun of one member stops being funny quickly.
