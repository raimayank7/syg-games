Ladies and kitty party group names

Names for the kitty party, the saheli circle and the ladies of the colony. Cheerful and respectful.

Tap a name to copy it. Then open your group, tap its name at the top and paste.

Make a name with your own word

Cheerful, respectful and easy to say

A kitty or ladies' circle is usually a mix of ages: a new bride in the colony, working women, mothers of school children and grandmothers who started the kitty twenty years ago. The name should sit comfortably with all of them. Words of friendship carry well across ages: saheli, sakhi, circle, club. Add the colony, the building or the day of the meeting to make it your own: "Sector 9 Saheli Circle", "Second Saturday Club".

If the group handles the kitty's money, turns and hosting dates, a plain second line in the group description helps more than a clever name: who hosts this month, the date and the contribution agreed by the members.

What to steer clear of

Avoid names that turn the members into a joke about gossip or shopping; many women find those labels tiring, and husbands and children see the name too. Avoid names that fix everyone as "aunties" or "bhabhis" if the group includes unmarried or younger members. Keep out anything about age, looks or weight. And since kitty groups often welcome a new neighbour, choose a name that an outsider can join without feeling she has walked into a private joke.

For the monthly meeting, the poll ideas settle dates and themes quickly, and a short welcome message makes a new member comfortable from day one.

How to choose a good group name

  • Say what the group is. A new member should understand it from the name.
  • Keep it short. Long names are cut off in the chat list; about 25 characters show fully.
  • Make it yours. Add a surname, a city, a year or a flat block: "Sharma Parivaar Express", "Block B Buzz".
  • Keep it kind. A name that makes fun of one member stops being funny quickly.
