Study group names

Names for study circles, tuition batches and exam preparation groups, to keep everyone serious with a smile.

Tap a name to copy it. Then open your group, tap its name at the top and paste.

Make a name with your own word

A name that sets the mood for work

A study group lives or dies by its mood. If the name sounds like a party, the chat becomes one; if it sounds like a punishment, nobody opens it. The names in this list sit in between: serious about the goal, light in tone. For an exam group, the strongest names carry the target and the date, because they remind every member why the group exists each time a message arrives: "NEET 2027: One Chapter a Day", "CA Inter May Batch".

For a tuition batch or a class project, put the subject and timing in the name ("Maths 6 pm Batch") so that students in three different tuitions do not post in the wrong one.

What to avoid

Do not promise ranks or marks in the name ("100 Percent Club"): it puts pressure on the members who are struggling, and they are the ones a study group should help most. Avoid names that mock a subject or a teacher. Keep the name short, since students often search for the group in a hurry before a test. And when the exam is over, either rename the group for the next goal or close it kindly; a dead study group with an old exam's name is a small daily sadness in the chat list.

Next, pin the study group rules, especially the one about doubts and the one about silence during study hours.

How to choose a good group name

  • Say what the group is. A new member should understand it from the name.
  • Keep it short. Long names are cut off in the chat list; about 25 characters show fully.
  • Make it yours. Add a surname, a city, a year or a flat block: "Sharma Parivaar Express", "Block B Buzz".
  • Keep it kind. A name that makes fun of one member stops being funny quickly.
