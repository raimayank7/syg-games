Travel and family games

Word games for car rides, train journeys, queues and power cuts. They need no board, no phone and no pen, only people who are willing to play. They are also a gentle way to practise spelling, memory and geography with children.

  • I SpyA looking game for car, bus and train journeys.Travel and family games · 2 or more players · 10 minutes
  • A to Z GamePlayers go around naming things in one category, in alphabetical order.Travel and family games · 2 or more players · 10 to 15 minutes
  • AtlasAtlas is the place-name version of antakshari.Travel and family games · 2 or more players · 10 to 20 minutes
