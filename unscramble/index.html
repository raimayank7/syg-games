Unscramble the Word

The host posts words with their letters jumbled. The first player to post the right word wins the point. It is quick, fair and good for children's spelling.

Players
2 or more
Time
10 minutes
You need
Nothing
Ages
8 and above
Type
WhatsApp group games

Who it suits and when to play

Unscramble suits families with school-age children, class groups and anybody who likes word puzzles. Children of eight and above can play if the words are short; choose longer words for adults. It is a quiet game, good for a time when members are free for only ten minutes, and easy to run in English or in Hindi words written in English letters.

How to play

  1. Tell the group the topic of the round, such as fruits or Indian cities.
  2. Post one jumbled word at a time.
  3. The first correct reply wins one point.
  4. After eight words, add up the points and name the winner.

Tips and variations

  • To make your own, write the word on paper and copy its letters in a new order. Count the letters twice.

Common problems and how to fix them

  • A jumble has more than one answer. Accept every real word that uses all the letters. Check your list for such doubles before you start.
  • The words are too hard and nobody answers. Give the first letter after one minute, and the theme after two.
  • One player wins everything. Give two points for the first right answer and one point to everyone else who answers within a minute.
