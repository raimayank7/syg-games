Find the Odd One

The host posts a block of the same emoji with one that is slightly different hidden inside. The first player to name its row and position wins. Small children can play this with their parents.

Players
2 or more
Time
5 minutes
You need
Nothing
Ages
5 and above
Type
WhatsApp group games

How to play

  1. Copy one of the puzzles below and post it.
  2. Players look for the emoji that is different.
  3. The first to reply with the right row and place, such as "Row 3, fourth", wins.
  4. Post the next puzzle.
