Name, Place, Animal, Thing

The old school-notebook game works very well on WhatsApp. One letter is called and everybody races to write a name, a place, an animal and a thing that start with it.

Players
2 or more
Time
15 minutes
You need
Phones, or paper and pens
Ages
7 and above
Type
WhatsApp group games

Who it suits and when to play

This is the game for groups that grew up playing it on the last page of a notebook: cousins, school batches and families with children of seven and above. It needs a little typing speed, so pair a small child or a grandparent with a partner. It is a good choice for a rainy evening, a long video call or the last period before the holidays.

How to play

  1. The host posts a letter, for example "Letter: M".
  2. Every player replies in one message with four words: a name, a place, an animal and a thing that begin with that letter.
  3. The round closes after 60 seconds, or when the first complete reply arrives, as you decide before starting.
  4. Score 10 points for a word nobody else wrote, 5 for a word shared with someone and 0 for a blank or wrong word.
  5. Play five letters. The highest total wins.

Ready message to start the game

Name, Place, Animal, Thing! I will post a letter. Reply in ONE message like this: Name: Meena Place: Mysuru Animal: Monkey Thing: Mirror You have 60 seconds. Unique answers get 10 points, shared answers 5.

Letters to call

  1. B
  2. M
  3. S
  4. P
  5. R
  6. T
  7. K
  8. G
  9. D
  10. C
  11. H
  12. L

Tips and variations

  • Skip hard letters such as Q, X and Z, or keep them for a bonus round.
  • Add more columns for older players: food, film-free topics such as flower, colour or profession.

Common problems and how to fix them

  • Players argue about whether a word is real. Name one judge before the first letter. A place must be findable on a map, and the judge decides in ten seconds.
  • Fast typists always finish first. Close each round on a timer instead of on the first reply, so that everybody gets the same 60 seconds.
  • People look answers up. Laugh about it and shorten the time to 40 seconds; nobody can search four words that quickly.
