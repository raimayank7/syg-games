Road Bingo

Each player has a card with nine or sixteen things that can be seen from a window. The first to spot a full line of them wins. It turns a long drive or train ride into a hunt, and children stop asking how far it is.

Make the cards at home before the trip. Every card should have the same things in a different order, so that the game is fair and no two players finish together by accident.

Players
2 or more
Time
30 minutes or the whole journey
You need
A card and a pencil for each player
Ages
6 and above
Type
Travel and family games

Who it suits and when to play

Road Bingo suits children from six upward on a car, bus or train journey of an hour or more, and adults often play their own card. It turns looking out of the window into a hunt. Make the cards before leaving home. For children who cannot read, draw the things instead of writing them.

How to play

  1. Draw a grid of three by three squares on a card for each player. For older children, use four by four.
  2. Write or draw one thing in each square, choosing from the list below. Mix easy things with one or two rare ones.
  3. On the journey, a player who sees a thing calls it out and crosses that square.
  4. Only the first player to call a particular sighting may cross it. The others wait for the next one.
  5. The first player to cross three squares in a line, across, down or corner to corner, shouts "Bingo" and wins the round.
  6. Swap cards and play again, or play on for the full card.

Things to spot on a road journey

  1. A tractor
  2. A petrol pump
  3. A bridge over a river
  4. A milestone with a number on it
  5. A dhaba with cots outside
  6. A yellow number plate
  7. A cow or buffalo
  8. A temple, mosque, gurdwara or church
  9. A truck with a painted back
  10. A bus with luggage on the roof
  11. A bicycle carrying two people
  12. A field with a scarecrow
  13. A railway crossing
  14. A water tank on a tower
  15. A person selling fruit
  16. A windmill or a line of electricity towers
  17. A school bus
  18. A hill or a mountain
  19. A flag
  20. A dog sleeping in the shade

Tips and variations

  • For a train journey, change the list: a station name board, a level crossing, a tunnel, a river bridge, a tea seller, a signal.
  • Team version for a full car: the left side of the car plays against the right side, each watching its own window.
  • Quiet version: players cross squares silently and show the card to the judge when they have a line.

Common problems and how to fix them

  • Two players spot the same thing. The first to call it out marks it. The other must find another one.
  • One square is never found. Agree that a card needs any one full line, not every square.
  • The driver is asked to look. The driver is the judge of disputes, by voice only, and never looks for things.
