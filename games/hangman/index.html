Guess the Word (Hangman)

One player thinks of a word and shows it as blanks. The others guess letters. Each wrong letter costs a life. On WhatsApp we count lives with hearts instead of drawing.

Players
2 or more
Time
10 minutes
You need
Nothing
Ages
8 and above
Type
WhatsApp group games

Who it suits and when to play

Guess the Word suits children who have begun to spell, from about eight, and adults who enjoy a quiet puzzle. On a chat it moves slowly and pleasantly, so it is a good game for an evening when members drop in and out. Teachers use it to revise spellings; families use it on journeys with a pen and the back of a ticket.

How to play

  1. The host thinks of a word and posts it as blanks, with the topic. For example: "_ _ _ _ _ _ (a fruit)".
  2. Players guess one letter at a time.
  3. For a right letter, the host posts the word again with that letter filled in.
  4. For a wrong letter, the host removes one heart from seven: ❤️❤️❤️❤️❤️❤️❤️.
  5. The group wins by finishing the word before the hearts run out. The player who completes it sets the next word.

Ready message to start the game

Guess the word! Topic: a fruit. _ _ _ _ _ _ Lives: ❤️❤️❤️❤️❤️❤️❤️ Guess one letter at a time.

Tips and variations

  • Start with vowels. Then try common letters such as R, S, T, N and L.
  • Use words everybody knows. Names of dishes, cities and animals work best.

Common problems and how to fix them

  • Nobody remembers which letters were tried. The host posts the tried letters with the word after every guess.
  • The word is too easy or too hard. Give the number of letters and a theme. Six to nine letters is the comfortable range.
  • Younger children do not like the hanging figure. Draw a flower losing petals or a ladder losing steps instead. The game is the same.
