Twenty Questions

One player thinks of an object. The others may ask up to twenty questions that can be answered only with yes or no, and try to work out what it is.

Players
2 or more
Time
10 minutes a round
You need
Nothing
Ages
8 and above
Type
WhatsApp group games

Who it suits and when to play

Twenty Questions suits thinkers of eight and above, and it is one of the few games that work just as well with two people as with twenty. Play it on a call, in a car or in a chat. It quietly teaches children to ask good questions, moving from wide ones to narrow ones, so teachers and parents like it as much as the players do.

How to play

  1. The thinker chooses a thing and tells the group only its type: a living thing, an object or a place.
  2. Players take turns to ask yes-or-no questions, such as "Is it bigger than a chair?"
  3. The thinker answers only "yes", "no" or "sometimes".
  4. Anyone may guess at any time, but a wrong guess counts as two questions.
  5. If nobody guesses within twenty questions, the thinker wins and reveals the answer.

Ready message to start the game

Twenty questions! I am thinking of an OBJECT found in most homes. Ask me yes-or-no questions. You have 20 in all. Question 1?

Tips and variations

  • Good first questions cut the choices in half: "Is it alive?", "Can I hold it in one hand?", "Is it found in the kitchen?"

Common problems and how to fix them

  • Players guess wildly instead of asking questions. Count a wrong guess as two questions. People become careful at once.
  • The answerer changes the object halfway. The answerer sends the word privately to one referee before the round starts.
  • The object is too hard for children. Agree on a family of things first, such as "something in a kitchen" or "an animal".
