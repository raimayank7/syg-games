Pick a Number

The host keeps a secret list of small, harmless tasks. Each member picks a number without knowing the task behind it, and then has to do it. The surprise is the fun.

Players
3 or more
Time
15 minutes
You need
Nothing
Ages
10 and above
Type
WhatsApp group games

Who it suits and when to play

Pick a Number suits friends' and cousins' groups where people are comfortable being a little silly, from about ten years and up. Each number hides a small task or question, so the host needs a list ready. It is not right for a formal office group or a group with members who are shy about sending voice notes or photos.

How to play

  1. Post the starting message and ask every member to choose a number from 1 to 20.
  2. When a member replies with a number, post the task for that number.
  3. The member completes the task in the group: a photo, a voice note or a message.
  4. A member who cannot do a task may swap it for another number, once.

Ready message to start the game

Game time! Pick any number from 1 to 20 and reply with it. Each number hides a small, fun task. No backing out!

Tasks for the numbers 1 to 20

  1. Send a photo of the view from your nearest window.
  2. Sing two lines of your favourite song in a voice note.
  3. Tell the group one thing you learned this week.
  4. Send a photo of your tea or coffee cup right now.
  5. Type the alphabet backwards as fast as you can.
  6. Say a tongue twister three times in a voice note.
  7. Share your most used emoji and explain why.
  8. Describe your day using only five emojis.
  9. Share a childhood nickname.
  10. Send the oldest photo of a place (not a person) from your gallery.
  11. Write a four-line poem about the person who messaged before you. Keep it kind.
  12. Tell the group your favourite dish and who cooks it best.
  13. Send a voice note of your best animal sound.
  14. Share one good habit you want to start.
  15. Draw the group admin on paper in one minute and send the photo.
  16. Tell a clean joke.
  17. Praise three members of the group, one line each.
  18. Recommend one song everyone should hear today. Only the name.
  19. Send a photo of something yellow near you.
  20. Share the last thing that made you laugh.

Tips and variations

  • Read the list first and remove any task that would not suit your group.
  • Never include tasks about money, private photos or teasing somebody. Fun should be fun for everyone.

Common problems and how to fix them

  • A player does not want to do the task. Always offer a second choice: an easy question in place of the task. Nobody should be pushed.
  • Everyone picks the same numbers. Strike off used numbers in the list and post the remaining ones after each turn.
  • Tasks need things people do not have. Keep tasks to what can be done from a chair with a phone: a voice note, a photo of the view, a typed tongue twister.
