This or That

A rapid-fire game of quick choices. The host posts pairs and everyone answers at once. It takes two minutes and tells you a lot about the group.

Players
2 or more
Time
5 to 10 minutes
You need
Nothing
Ages
6 and above
Type
WhatsApp group games

Who it suits and when to play

This is the lightest game on the site: one tap, one word, done. It suits very large groups, very busy groups and groups where many members only read. Use it as a warm-up before a longer game, or as a daily question in a team or family group. Children enjoy it from about six, especially when they can argue for their choice.

How to play

  1. Post five pairs in one message, numbered 1 to 5.
  2. Everyone replies with their five choices in one message.
  3. Count which side won each pair and post the result.
  4. Find the two members with the most matching answers and name them "twins of the day".

This or that? Reply with your 5 picks in one message. 1. Chai or coffee 2. Mountains or beach 3. Morning or night 4. Sweet or spicy 5. Call or text

Tips and variations

  • Use WhatsApp polls if your group likes tapping more than typing.

Common problems and how to fix them

  • Only a few people reply. Post it as a poll if your chat app has polls. People who never type will still vote.
  • Someone refuses to choose and says "both". Allow it once, with a smile, then make "no both" the rule.
  • It becomes a real argument. Remind everybody that the point is the difference of tastes, and move to the next pair.
