Memory Tray

Players look at a tray of objects for one minute. Then the tray is covered and they write down everything they can remember. It is a quiet game that works well between two noisy ones.

Players
2 or more
Time
10 minutes
You need
A tray, 15 small objects, a cloth, paper and pens
Ages
6 and above
Type
Games for kids

Who it suits and when to play

Memory Tray suits children from six upward and is a quiet favourite at birthday parties, in classrooms and on evenings when the power is out. It needs no reading, only looking and remembering, and adults are often beaten by nine-year-olds. Use fewer objects for small children and more for older ones.

How to play

  1. Put 12 to 15 small things on a tray: a spoon, a key, a comb, a coin, a leaf, an eraser and so on.
  2. Show the tray for exactly one minute. No writing yet.
  3. Cover the tray with a cloth.
  4. Give two minutes to write down as many objects as each player can remember.
  5. Uncover the tray and check. One point for each correct object, minus one for an object that was never there.

Tips and variations

  • Second round: remove one object secretly and ask what is missing.
  • For small children use 8 objects; for adults use 20.

Common problems and how to fix them

  • Children call out answers. Everyone writes or draws their list silently. Lists are read out only at the end.
  • It is too hard for the youngest. Use eight objects for under-sevens, and let them say the names to an adult who writes.
  • The same objects become boring. Change half the tray each round, or remove one object and ask which is missing.
