Leader Says

The leader gives quick commands. Players must obey only the commands that begin with the words "Leader says". It sounds easy until the commands get fast.

Players
3 or more
Time
10 minutes
You need
Nothing
Ages
4 and above
Type
Games for kids

Who it suits and when to play

Leader Says suits children from four to ten, in a classroom, at a party or in a park, and it needs nothing at all. It trains listening, which is why teachers use it to settle a class. Three players are enough, and thirty is no problem. Let the children take turns at being the leader; that is the part they like best.

How to play

  1. One player is the leader and stands in front.
  2. The leader calls commands such as "Leader says touch your nose".
  3. Players do the action only when the command starts with "Leader says".
  4. A player who follows a command without those words, or is too slow, is out.
  5. The last player left becomes the next leader.

Commands to call

  1. Touch your nose
  2. Hop on one foot
  3. Clap three times
  4. Turn around
  5. Touch your toes
  6. Wave both hands
  7. Sit down
  8. Stand like a tree
  9. Pat your head
  10. Jump twice
  11. Close one eye
  12. March on the spot

Tips and variations

  • The leader can trick players by doing a different action from the one called.

Common problems and how to fix them

  • The same children are always out first. Being out lasts for one round only, or the player does three jumps and comes back in.
  • The leader gives unsafe commands. Agree on the rule: nothing that involves running into others, climbing or touching someone else.
  • Children copy the leader's action instead of listening. That is the game. Slow down for the smallest players and praise careful listening.
