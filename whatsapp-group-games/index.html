Group Games

WhatsApp group games

Games that are played by typing, tapping and sending photos or voice notes. Nobody needs to be in the same room, so they suit family groups, friends in different cities, school batches and society groups. Most need only a host who posts a ready message and keeps the score. Start with a quick one such as This or That, and keep the longer games for a weekend evening when most members are free.

  • Emoji GuessThe quizmaster posts a few emojis and the group guesses the dish, place or festival they stand for.WhatsApp group games · 3 or more players · 10 to 20 minutes
  • Name, Place, Animal, ThingThe old school-notebook game works very well on WhatsApp.WhatsApp group games · 2 or more players · 15 minutes
  • Two Truths and a LieEach player shares three statements about themselves.WhatsApp group games · 3 or more players · 15 to 30 minutes
  • Would You RatherPost a funny choice between two things and ask everyone to pick one and say why.WhatsApp group games · 2 or more players · 10 to 20 minutes
  • This or ThatA rapid-fire game of quick choices.WhatsApp group games · 2 or more players · 5 to 10 minutes
  • Pick a NumberThe host keeps a secret list of small, harmless tasks.WhatsApp group games · 3 or more players · 15 minutes
  • Unscramble the WordThe host posts words with their letters jumbled.WhatsApp group games · 2 or more players · 10 minutes
  • Story ChainThe group writes one story together, one line at a time.WhatsApp group games · 3 or more players · 15 to 30 minutes
  • Photo Scavenger HuntThe host names an object and the players run around their homes to find it and send a photo.WhatsApp group games · 3 or more players · 15 to 20 minutes
  • Twenty QuestionsOne player thinks of an object.WhatsApp group games · 2 or more players · 10 minutes a round
  • Guess the Word (Hangman)One player thinks of a word and shows it as blanks.WhatsApp group games · 2 or more players · 10 minutes
  • Find the Odd OneThe host posts a block of the same emoji with one that is slightly different hidden inside.WhatsApp group games · 2 or more players · 5 minutes
  • Guess the MemberMembers send the host one little-known, harmless fact about themselves.WhatsApp group games · 5 or more players · 15 minutes
  • Caption ThisThe host posts a funny, harmless photo and everybody writes a caption for it.WhatsApp group games · 3 or more players · 10 minutes
  • Fastest Finger FirstOne question at a time, and only the first right reply scores.WhatsApp group games · 3 or more players · 10 minutes
