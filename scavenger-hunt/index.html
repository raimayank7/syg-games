Photo Scavenger Hunt

The host names an object and the players run around their homes to find it and send a photo. It gets everyone off the sofa, and children love it.

Players
3 or more
Time
15 to 20 minutes
You need
Phones with a camera
Ages
6 and above
Type
WhatsApp group games

Who it suits and when to play

A photo scavenger hunt suits groups whose members are in different homes or cities but free at the same time: a family on a Sunday morning, cousins in the holidays, a remote team on a fun Friday. Children love it from about six, with an adult's phone. Choose it when people need to get up and move; it is the opposite of a sitting game.

How to play

  1. Announce the rules: one item at a time, photos must be taken now, not from the gallery.
  2. Post the first item, such as "something round and red".
  3. The first photo that fits wins one point. The host decides close calls.
  4. Play ten items. Ask players not to run on stairs or climb to reach anything.

Ready message to start the game

Scavenger hunt! I will name a thing. Find it at home and send a PHOTO taken right now. The first correct photo gets the point. Ready? Item 1: something round and red.

Things to find

  1. Something round and red
  2. A spoon bigger than your hand
  3. A book with a blue cover
  4. Something older than you
  5. A key that is not a house key
  6. Three different leaves
  7. A coin from before the year 2010
  8. Something that makes a sound
  9. A photo frame
  10. A sock without its pair
  11. Something with stripes
  12. A plant in a pot
  13. A handwritten note or letter
  14. Something that smells nice
  15. A toy
  16. Something made of wood
  17. A calendar
  18. Four things of the same colour, in one photo
  19. A fruit or vegetable that is green
  20. Your favourite cup

Tips and variations

  • For a calmer version, post the whole list and give ten minutes. The player with the most items wins.

Common problems and how to fix them

  • Players send old photos from the gallery. Ask for something in each photo that proves it is new, such as a spoon or today's newspaper.
  • Someone does not have an item. Allow a drawing of the item, for half a point. It usually gets the biggest laugh.
  • Children run or climb to find things. Say at the start that nothing on the list needs running, climbing or leaving the house.
