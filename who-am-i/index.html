Who Am I?

Each player has a word stuck on their forehead that everyone else can see. By asking yes-or-no questions, players try to find out what they are.

Players
3 or more
Time
15 minutes
You need
Sticky notes or paper and tape
Ages
7 and above
Type
Party games

How to play

  1. Write the name of an animal, an object or a profession on a sticky note for each player.
  2. Stick a note on each player's forehead without letting them read it.
  3. In turn, a player asks the group one yes-or-no question, such as "Am I alive?" or "Am I found in a kitchen?"
  4. After a "yes", the same player may ask again. After a "no", the turn passes.
  5. The first player to name their own word wins. Play on until all have guessed.

Tips and variations

  • With children, choose only animals. They can also act out hints for one another.
