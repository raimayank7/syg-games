Langdi

Langdi is a hopping tag game that is very popular in Maharashtra. One chaser hops on one foot and tries to tag the runners of the other team inside a marked square.

Players
6 or more, in two teams
Time
15 to 20 minutes
You need
Open ground
Ages
7 and above
Type
Traditional Indian games

How to play

  1. Mark a square of about ten big steps on each side. Make two teams.
  2. The defending team sends three or four runners into the square.
  3. The chasing team sends one chaser, who must hop on one foot the whole time.
  4. A runner who is tagged, or who steps outside the square, is out.
  5. If the chaser puts the other foot down, the chaser is out and the next chaser hops in.
  6. When the time is up, usually five minutes, count the runners tagged. The teams change roles. The team with more tags wins.

Tips and variations

  • It is a great workout for balance. Let chasers change the hopping foot only between turns.
